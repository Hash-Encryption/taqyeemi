Our story

A dedication to the art of baking.

At PEKARNA, great food starts with exceptional ingredients and patient technique. In Jeddah’s Al Khalidiyah district, French pastry tradition meets the everyday comfort of warm artisanal bread.

Stop by for a morning croissant and a perfectly poured coffee, or share one of our signature “Tina” pizzas with friends. Everything is crafted with care in our kitchen.
